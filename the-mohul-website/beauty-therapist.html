Beauty Therapy, Done Right

More Than Just a Facial

Beauty therapy at The Mohul is not a 15-minute assembly-line facial. Every session starts with skin analysis — we look at your skin's current state, what it needs today, and choose products & techniques accordingly.

Our therapists are certified in classic and modern techniques: European facial massage, lymphatic drainage, pressure point therapy, and product layering based on skin type. We use dermatologist-tested products from premium brands.

Hygiene is non-negotiable. Fresh towels, sanitized tools, single-use applicators, and a spotless setup for every single client. Read our full Hygiene & Safety Practices.
