Rescheduling Policy

Free rescheduling subject to availability. Request must be made at least 48 hours before the original date. If peak-season new date is unavailable, a full refund or another date will be offered.

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the same payment method. A ₹200 processing fee may apply. A confirmation message will be sent.
