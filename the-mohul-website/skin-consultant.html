What is Skin Consultation?

Know Your Skin, Then Choose Right

Skin consultation at The Mohul is a deep, personalized session where we analyze your skin type, identify concerns (acne, pigmentation, dryness, aging, sensitivity), and understand your lifestyle, diet, and current skincare routine.

Based on this analysis, we craft a custom skincare plan — what products to use, in what order, how often, and what to avoid. We also flag when a concern needs a dermatologist's attention rather than over-the-counter products.

This is not a sales pitch for products. Our recommendations are based on what your skin actually needs — whether that's a ₹200 cleanser or a simple lifestyle change.
