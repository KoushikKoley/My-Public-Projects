The Story

From Makeup to a Full Beauty Studio

What started in 2018 as a single-makeup-artist service has grown into The Mohul Makeup & Beauty Studio — a complete beauty destination in Bankura offering skin consultation, beauty therapy, salon services, makeup artistry, and a team of professional MUAs.

Trained under senior Kolkata MUAs and certified in HD, airbrush, and beauty therapy, The Mohul specializes in looks and treatments that are not just beautiful — but bulletproof against Indian weather conditions.

Every client gets personalized attention, premium products, and a commitment to punctuality that's rare in this industry. Our 4.9 Justdial rating isn't bought — it's earned, one happy client at a time.
